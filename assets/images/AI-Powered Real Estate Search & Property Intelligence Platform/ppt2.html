Market Friction

Client Problem's

Real estate decisions demand instant answers, but property intelligence is fragmented.

Critical Pain Points
01

Fragmented Data

Critical data is scattered across disconnected silos: assessment databases, zoning shapefiles, school boundary maps, transit feeds, and census sources.

Zoning Maps Tax DB Transit
02

Slow Manual Workflow

Agents and analysts waste hours cross-referencing multiple systems. Jargon-heavy government tools create bottlenecks for rapid decision making.

Productivity Drain
03

High Error Risk

Human error is inevitable when manually collating complex data. Mistakes in taxes, valuation, zoning, or compliance boundaries carry massive liability.

Impacts: Valuation accuracy & Compliance

04

AI Hallucinations

Traditional LLMs invent plausible but false facts. In real estate, "guessing" a zoning code or lot size destroys brand trust and is legally unacceptable.

Not Audit Ready
05

User Experience Friction

Customers don't want complex search forms with 50+ filters. They want to ask a simple natural language question and get an instant, accurate answer.

Complex Filter UI
Steep Learning Curve
